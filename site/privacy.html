Privacy

Your work stays on your Mac

The VoxStage software

  • No account, no telemetry. VoxStage does not collect usage data, crash reports or analytics, and has nowhere to send them.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Scripts, recordings, voices, generated audio and exports are stored in the user-data folder of your copy and never leave the Mac unless you move them.
  • What does use the network: the setup scripts download the models from their publishers (Hugging Face), and the dependency tools (Homebrew, uv, npm) download software. After setup VoxStage runs offline: the launcher starts it with the model libraries held offline (HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1).
  • Network access mode is off by default. When you turn it on, other devices on the same Wi-Fi can open VoxStage after typing an access key; the key stays in user-data/lan-key.txt on your Mac. It is plain HTTP inside your own network, so use it only on a network you trust.
  • Packages you make hold the whole project, including any recording you supplied. Keep them to yourself unless everyone whose voice is in them agrees.

This website

  • It is a set of static pages: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no forms and no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) for security and operation, under its own policy.
  • If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you.
